= Welcome to Infinicorp - we need your help to test our new MCP workflows!

.Infinicorp Logo
image::lb1305_M1_Infinicorp_Logo.png[link=self, window=blank, width=100%]

We need your help to update Infinicorp’s management of their Red Hat Enterprise Linux environments.

Infinicorp Robotics Solutions, headquartered here in Atlanta, GA, is a large multi-national corporation focused on electronics manufacturing across a variety of industries.  We got our start selling vending machines for a wide variety of products in Las Vegas, but over time we have branched out into the field of robotics and have a goal of being the leading world wide seller and distributor of autonomous robots.


Infinicorp has some challenges around the number of management interfaces that we have across our disparate systems.  Our technical staff are used to working in a terminal and don’t want to be bothered to go to a variety of different user interfaces to get critical systems information.

Infinicorp would like to leverage model content protocol or MCP servers to allow these administrators the options to use natural language to ask questions and get information from the terminal interfaces where they are already working.

.The setup:
[%collapsible]
====
In our typical IT environment we might be using cursor or vscode as the “front door”, but in the unclassified environment that you will be working in for testing we have a terminal with goose.  

Your unclassified segmented environment contains the following: 


* One (1) preconfigured mcp host
* One (1) preconfigured Satellite server
* Two (2) RHEL hosts connected to console.redhat.com
* Two (2) RHEL hosts connected to Satellite


Preconfigured for you on the mcp host are the following:

* https://github.com/RedHatInsights/insights-mcp/blob/main/README.md[Red Hat Lightspeed model context protocol (mcp) server^]
* https://docs.redhat.com/en/documentation/red_hat_satellite/6.18/html/managing_hosts/connecting-ai-applications-to-the-mcp-server-for-satellite[Red Hat Satellite mcp server^]
* https://rhel-lightspeed.github.io/linux-mcp-server/[Red Hat Enterprise Linux mcp server^]

You will be using *goose* as your local AI agent.  https://block.github.io/goose/[Goose^] is an open source, extensible AI agent. +
Goose is configured to use LiteLLM as a provider, with qwen3 as a model. +
This is all pre-configured for you in the segmented network that Infinicorp is providing.
